# Milestone 6 — Separately authored artwork LOD and detail editing

## Confirmed direction — 2026-10-30
- Automatic “layer zoom” means **artwork-layer LOD**, not automatically switching independent map documents. Enable it by default, with an optional disable control.
- World hexes share the continuous chunked board in [[E-002]]. Empty hexes are not blocked by missing document assignments.
- Detail levels are **separately authored**. Their coordinate relationships and presentation must remain coherent.
- Keep an explicit editing target; automatic presentation changes must not retarget an in-progress gesture.
- **Retire the battlemat/battlemap concept.** Its useful content is another authored detail view of the local map, not a separate document or movable canvas container.
- The previously proposed battlemat bounds/content-following resize policy is superseded. Projection framing is a separate presentation concern in [[E-007]] and [[T-013]].

These are confirmed requirements, including the user's later revision that replaces the earlier local-battlemat-object proposal. Exact LOD thresholds, editing-target presentation and detail data structures remain design work.
