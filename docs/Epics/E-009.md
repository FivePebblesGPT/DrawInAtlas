# Drawing / Atlas capability separation

## Problem and objective
The 2026-10-30 manual review rejected usability even though component tests passed. The current folder layout already separates artwork, editing, campaign, navigation and persistence, but the user-facing capabilities are still coupled: `AtlasCanvas` handles drawing and map/navigation/presentation interactions; `AtlasArtworkEditor` depends on that canvas; drawing commands mutate through `AtlasCampaign`; and preview capture instantiates the interactive canvas.

**Goal:** deliver a useful Drawing-only vertical slice and make Atlas consume it via explicit, testable contracts, without a big-bang rewrite.

## Status
**PROPOSED — architectural decisions not yet approved.** `T-051` is a mandatory decision gate; no ticket below authorizes an implementation change until the relevant choices are answered.

## Work sequence
1. [[T-051|Approve capability boundaries, ownership and compatibility contracts]].
2. [[T-052|Extract a campaign-independent Drawing core and surface contract]].
3. [[T-053|Create a Drawing-only workspace and separate rendering/input]] and [[T-054|Decouple drawing edit commands while retaining integrated history]]; these may progress in small parallel changes after T-052.
4. [[T-055|Embed Drawing into Atlas through adapters, overlays and read-only capture]].
5. [[T-056|Prove saves, behavior and performance; retire shims only after acceptance]].

## Architectural hypotheses for review (not decisions)
- Keep one repository, Godot project and application initially, with two separately usable capability modules rather than immediately maintaining two products.
- Drawing must not depend on `AtlasCampaign`, `AtlasMapDocument`, `AtlasGrid`, map navigation, tokens/fog or projector controllers; Atlas may depend on Drawing's public contracts.
- Drawing owns board-space artwork, raster/Objects tools, selections, gestures, rendering and authored-detail data. Atlas owns campaigns, floor/map organization, grids, links and discovery. Ownership of LOD policy, history and presentation overlays remains open in T-051.
- Preserve the current native `.scn` contract and serialized Resource identities during extraction; treat any schema/resource-path migration as a separate explicit approval.
- Existing E-001..E-008 feature tickets remain authoritative for features; do not duplicate, complete or expand them through an architecture refactor.

## Out of scope
No new collaboration/projector features, no destructive legacy map conversion, no mass namespace rename, no forced alternative save format, and no claim of usability acceptance from automated test success.

## Epic acceptance
- A Drawing-only scene can create/edit/render artwork without loading a campaign or map/navigation stack.
- Atlas can open the same artwork through a documented adapter; one-edit-at-a-time, masks, floors, auto artwork-LOD and map links continue to behave as specified.
- The Drawing source dependency audit excludes Atlas domain/UI classes, except explicitly documented temporary shims outside the extracted module.
- Native save/load/backup and Web export regression gates pass; owner reviews representative pointer/keyboard workflows.
- Changes are reviewed in independently reversible increments, not one all-or-nothing refactor.
