# T-251 — Dock toggle owned by PanelDock, not the canvas seam

**Date:** 2026-11-22. **Branch:** `sprint/2026-11-20-selection-dock-colors-brushes`.
**Base remote commit:** `7b1fd306` (user's original concept/current screenshots are
present under `docs/assets/drawing-ux-2026-11-22/`). **Review:** The owner
rejected the preceding UX. No physical or owner approval is inferred.

## Diagnosis and correction

Previously `_make_dock_toggle` added a `top_level` Button to the workspace
root; `_layout_dock_toggle` placed it at `inspector.x - toggle.width + 2`,
within the editing canvas, while rail icons started at the top of their
scrollable VBox. This generated overlapping pointer targets. The original
T-222 isolated GUI witnesses did not test a dock-owned flow slot.

- `workspace.gd` now owns one `DockToggleSlot` as the **first 48×56 child** of
  `DockIconRail` (VBoxContainer). In minimized mode, this visible slot
  participates in container layout, and the same Button is its normal child;
  the first icon begins below the entire toggle hit rectangle.
- In expanded mode, the slot is hidden (no vertical row). The same Button is
  a `top_level` child of `PanelDock`, anchored to its own global top-left;
  `DockPanelGutter` adds **34px horizontally only** to all scrollable panels,
  keeping the 30×56 Button over the dock with zero canvas overlap. The
  expanded minimum is fixed at **226px** (prior 192px + 34px gutter) so
  floating Colors cannot reduce the dock's intrinsic width and resize the canvas. No extra
  always-visible text button or floating host is introduced.
- On rerenders `_render_dock` preserves the slot and its Button, and deferred
  focus restoration compensates for Godot dropping GUI focus on reparent.
  The new test initially failed on keyboard focus after first Enter; fixed
  without changing panel history, model ownership or settings format.

### Regression diagnosis while closing the ticket

The first expanded horizontal gutter made `PanelDock`'s intrinsic minimum
width change from 218px to 208px when Colors was floated, shifting the
canvas by 10px and failing four T-081 expectations. Pinning the expanded
minimum to 226px restores the preexisting invariant; T-081 then passes
58/58. T-080 initially missed a narrow focus/drag preview after the gutter
change, but passes 70/70 with the stable minimum; its remaining nested
ScrollContainer focus warning is recorded rather than hidden. The 200%
software-DPI pointer regression also uncovered that a *floating test fixture*
straddled the new dock at that tiny logical window size; positioning the
fixture clear of the dock makes the actual toggle hit target testable without
claiming arbitrary overlapping floating hosts can never occlude controls.
