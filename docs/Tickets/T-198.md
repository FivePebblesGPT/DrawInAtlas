## Findings and limits

[Matched-frame report](../evidence/T-198-2026-11-19-matched-callbacks.md)
retains provenance and individual counts, and
[compact machine-readable evidence](../evidence/T-198-2026-11-19-matched-callbacks.json)
can be reproduced from the existing raw T-197 sources. **541** adjacent
callback pairs can be verified, with no ≥83.333ms period in those pairs;
**251** adjacency pairs lack a matched callback, so their timing is unknown.

same four full-workspace processes. These global intervals are **not**
therefore 19 proved slow painting frames, but this diagnostic **does not
prove** all active frame gaps are short or physical FPS is adequate.
