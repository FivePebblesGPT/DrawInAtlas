# 96px/100-event 200ms stretch and paced full-workspace frame tails

**Motivation:** T-194 achieved a narrow 250ms p95 *software-X11 benchmark* milestone in two reversed-order same-binary A/B pairs with a 20-segment native batch cap. It did **not** reach the 200ms stretch goal, establish population-tail confidence, improve 54%/100% full-workspace frame/release tails consistently or provide owner hardware acceptance. See [T-194 evidence](../evidence/T-194-2026-11-17-native-batch-cadence.md).

## One bounded deliverable

- [x] Re-baseline the exact full native 96px, 512², 100-event gesture under the current 20-segment cap, plus native Brush/Eraser, cold/warm/held input at 54% and 100%, including first postdraw, queued release→settled, RSS, per-frame and pending-input tails.
- [x] Identify an independently measured wall-clock culprit; develop and fail-first test **one** reversible optimization toward the **200ms stretch**, not a bookkeeping reduction without a proven gesture/frame benefit.
- [x] Demonstrate exact Hokusai native source SHA, coverage, all samples and interpolation, 6ms soft stage deadline, preview, undo/redo, cancel, selection, hidden RGB, portable Save/Open and Atlas parity. Run reversed-order same-release-binary A/B with confirmed zero exits. Preserve rejected variants and raw failures.
- [x] Publish p50/p95 and limitations; do not claim physical mouse-to-photon or population FPS from virtual X11. If 200ms or consistent frame pacing remain unachieved, report the precise blocker without manufacturing acceptance.
- [x] Keep the independent T-095 physical owner review, T-151/T-152 consolidation, T-148/T-149 native transition and T-105 Windows/export gates open; no agent-approved manual signoff.

## Delivered (2026-11-17)

Completed a bounded, reversible **release-only second drain** with a real
fail-first graphical regression. Captured input retains the 20-segment native
transaction cap; after release, `_process()` may invoke at most one additional
`_drain_segments()` with the same **6ms soft deadline per call**, never a hard
per-frame or device latency guarantee. The existing 16-segment T-194
counterfactual still passes. Set `DRAWING_T195_RELEASE_CATCHUP_COUNTERFACTUAL=1`
to disable the extra drain against the **same checkout and release native binary**.

[Full evidence, raw logs, failed candidates and SHA-256 manifest](../evidence/T-195-2026-11-17-release-catchup.md).
The two reversed-order strict 100-event A/B pairs each have two cold processes
and two warm gestures per mode, all confirmed zero exits and identical Hokusai
native source SHA. Pooled reference→optimized `total_to_postdraw_ms` is cold
p50 **239.2→214.5**, p95 **303.5→252.6**; warm p50 **228.0→200.8**, p95
**277.9→209.4** (nearest-rank max of four observations). Source coverage,
input event count, interpolation, selection/hidden RGB, preview, history,
cancellation, standalone Save/Open and Atlas integration remain valid.
The independent full-workspace A/B includes four confirmed zero-exit processes
covering cold/warm/held Brush and Eraser at 54% and 100%; frame/release tails
remain **mixed**, with long frames surviving. The cold p95 exceeds even the
250ms earlier small-sample milestone by 2.6ms here; neither 200ms stretch nor
owner physical input-to-photon performance is established.

The direct graphical regression passes **85/85**, the complete 11-suite
graphical target and full offline `make check` both exit 0. The rejected
28- and 24-segment release-cap candidates, fail-first failures, cold/warm
raw logs, RSS and full-workspace frame/backlog traces remain in evidence.
