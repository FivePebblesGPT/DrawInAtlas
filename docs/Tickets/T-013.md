# Move and resize canvas objects with explicit drawing behavior

## Source
[[ManualReview_2026-10-30#Usability testing|Usability testing]], items 10 and 12; ownership contract: [[T-012]].

## Acceptance
- A local battlemat is selected, dragged, moved and resized as a canvas object.
- The user explicitly chooses whether associated drawings move with it or remain in place.
- Define resize/content-resampling behavior rather than silently transforming artwork.
- Operations respect masks/layer protections, are undoable and survive saving.
- Demonstrate bounds-only and content-following operations, including cancellation and negative-direction drags.
