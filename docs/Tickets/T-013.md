# Frame the projector viewport with a GM-board object

## Source
[[ManualReview_2026-10-30#Usability testing|Usability testing]], items 10 and 12, superseded by the user's later projection-object decision in [[E-007]]. This ticket retains its ID but replaces the retired battlemat move/resize work.

## Acceptance
- Display a selectable framing object on the GM board matching the active projector output's aspect ratio.
- Moving it pans the player camera; enlarging/shrinking it zooms out/in by showing more/less board.
- Its manipulation does not move, resize, resample or reassign artwork.
- Keep framing separate from output-window dimensions and physical projector/grid calibration.
- Respect the player privacy contract and agree persistence, cancellation, follow/freeze and history semantics before implementation.
- Demonstrate framing controls through real native input.
