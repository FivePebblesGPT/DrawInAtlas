# Retire battlemat creation controls

## Source
[[ManualReview_2026-10-30#Usability testing|Usability testing]], item 10.

The user's later decision in [[E-006]] retires the concept, superseding the earlier plan to restrict creation to local maps.

## Acceptance
- Remove “Drag battlemap” and other battlemat creation affordances from tools, menus, shortcuts and pointer paths at every detail level.
- Local detail is accessed through the authored-detail/LOD workflow, not through creation of another canvas/container.
- Do not replace the removed tool with an equivalent under another name.
- Preserve ordinary artwork editing; projection framing is separately scheduled in [[T-013]].
