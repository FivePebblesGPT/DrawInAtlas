## Defensive link-action follow-up — 2026-11-01 (acceptance still pending)

The existing **Linked maps…** form already snapshots an entrance before
**Apply**, but its **Unlink**, **Focus**, and **Enter** buttons previously acted on
the *current* record even if the form had been opened before another edit. In
particular, **Unlink** could delete a changed entrance and its orphaned
placement without the operator seeing the changed values.

- Form-driven actions now compare the open form's campaign/source floor and
  entrance/placement snapshot against the current graph. A stale form rejects
  Unlink/Focus/Enter, leaves the dialog and graph intact, and shows a visible
  error. Opening the form again refreshes its snapshot; direct trusted actions
  outside an open form retain their existing behavior.
- Apply also rejects a stale source context, including the **new marker** path
  whose generated entrance ID cannot match the form's empty selection ID.
- `linked_maps_ui_smoke.gd` exercises stale entrance and footprint changes,
  no history entry after rejection, no camera movement/navigation, and a
  successful fresh Unlink after reopening.

This is a safety improvement to the existing legacy-linked-map UX, **not**
acceptance of its terminology, discoverability, or its place in the final
shared-board model. No new links, battlemat containers, schema, or automated
map conversion were introduced. The ticket remains unchecked pending the
postponed product and hands-on acceptance decisions above.
