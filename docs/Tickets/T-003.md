# Align transparent detail previews with the hex overlay

## Source
[[ManualReview_2026-10-30#Usability testing|Usability testing]], item 1.

## Work
Replace the floating opaque-picture experience with correctly registered detail on the continuous board. Decide which compositing/detail presentation is appropriate after the canvas/LOD contract is established.

## Acceptance
- Detail is aligned to its actual board/hex position and remains registered while panning/zooming.
- Transparent regions do not acquire an unintended opaque rectangle.
- Preview framing/zoom represents the relevant detail rather than a detached arbitrarily scaled picture.
- Verify adjacent hexes, grid rotation/scale, chunk seams and empty detail; do not reintroduce a separate document per hex.
