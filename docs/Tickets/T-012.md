# Replace battlemats with separately authored local detail

## Source and confirmed constraints
[[ManualReview_2026-10-30#Usability testing|Usability testing]], items 10–12, followed by the user's revision in [[E-006]]. Battlemaps are simply more detailed local-map views; the battlemat concept is retired entirely. This supersedes the earlier within-map-container proposal.

## Acceptance
- Define how separately authored local detail is represented and selected within the shared-board/LOD model.
- Preserve explicit editing targets, continuous coordinates and independent artwork authoring across detail groups.
- Remove reliance on battlemat documents, containers or nesting as the way to access local detail.
- Identify migration/cleanup responsibilities for existing data, coordinated with [[T-015]].
- Keep projection framing in [[T-013]] separate from artwork ownership and transforms.
