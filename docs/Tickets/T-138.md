# Retain focal textures during complete over-budget detached previews

**Done — 2026-11-15, bounded cache-retention engineering follow-up only.**
T-103 retains the nearest 144 visible committed tiles in over-budget views,
but its focal planner returned no plan for every detached Brush/Move session.
Even with T-136's complete preview index, a pan relied on per-tile eviction
and recreated more far-edge textures than necessary. T-137's cold scheduling
correctly declines to defer *any* tiles when more than 144 must be shown; this
ticket does not change that decision.
