# Prioritize focal selection outlines when the 4096-tile budget overflows

**Done — 2026-11-15, bounded engineering correction only.** T-139 indexes
sparse explicit `true` selection pixels exactly when at most 4096 contour
tiles are visible. With larger indexes or compound/inverted masks, the
conservative fallback formerly enumerated tiles in top-left row-major order.
On a wide 25%-zoom viewport, the whole 4096-tile budget could be exhausted
before the selected contour near the camera focus was ever traced. A full
existing cache also could not reprioritize when the camera moved inside an
unchanged clipped selection tile window.

## Acceptance

- [x] Keep exact occupied-tile enumeration for positive explicit masks of at
  most 4096 tiles. Only the overflow/compound/inverted fallback changes.
- [x] Visit visible contour tiles in deterministic, clipped square rings
  outward from the camera focus; clamp an outside focus to the footprint and
  limit both traversal and retained caches to 4096 tiles. Do not enumerate
  vast offscreen coordinates or assume the enclosing bounds equal mask truth.
- [x] When a capped candidate plan changes focal tile but retains the same
  visible selection tile window, clear the stale contour cache and repopulate
  at most four new contours per frame. Preserve actual contour generation,
  canonical RGBA8 source, selection authorization and document revision.
- [x] Demonstrate failure on unchanged T-139 source and success on the same
  headless and native X11 framebuffer tests, including signed/moved camera,
  degenerate tall footprint, visible focal pixels and saturated cache.
- [x] Run neighboring T-139, T-088 clipboard/sparse and T-096 large-selection
  oracles, native selection witnesses, strict log checks, Python/CI/typing
  contracts, independent native-absent Drawing consumer, and ticket-store
  validation. Add to the existing T-088 required CI step and optional native
  selection workflow without changing the **46 unique CI check IDs**.

[Exact commands, retained raw logs, negative controls and boundaries](../evidence/T-140-2026-11-15-focal-outline-overflow.md).

**Limitations and review:** This deliberately chooses **which** 4096 tiles
are eligible to be progressively drawn; it does not trace all tiles in an
over-capacity selection and does not prove a faster dense selection. The
selection channel and file format are unmodified. A real physical reference
machine, hosted CI, and owner-facing selection/Move acceptance were not
verified. T-088 remains **In Progress** and T-096 **Manual Review**. No manual
approvals are granted by this ticket.
